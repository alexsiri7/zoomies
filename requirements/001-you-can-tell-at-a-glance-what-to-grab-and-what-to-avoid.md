## Why

Alex finds it hard to tell kibble and other things you should collect apart from obstacles you should dodge or jump. Mid-run there is no time to work it out, so you lose lives to things that looked like pickups, or skip pickups that looked like hazards.

## What

Everything you should collect looks and moves clearly differently from everything you should avoid, so a player can tell them apart instantly at full speed on a phone.

- Collectables share one bright, warm colour family and a glow or outline that no obstacle uses, and they move constantly (bob, spin or pulse).
- Obstacles use a contrasting, darker or cooler colour family, stay still, and never glow; tall dodge-only obstacles look distinct from low jumpable ones.
- The difference holds in all three levels (hallway, garden, rooftops at night) and still reads for colour-blind players, so it does not rely on red versus green alone.
- Collecting gives a clear positive flash or sound; hitting an obstacle gives a clearly different one.
